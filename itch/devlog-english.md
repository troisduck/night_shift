# Night Shift now speaks English

Night Shift is now playable in English. If your browser isn't set to Russian, the game starts in English on its own. You can also switch language with the button by the door or in the settings.

Everything is translated: Palych's lines, the briefings, the shift log, the tooltips and the labels on the panel itself. The buttons now read SCRAM, HEATERS and SPRAY, and the pumps are RCP-1 to RCP-4. That's about 630 lines in all.

Some of it couldn't be translated word for word. The third shift had a Soviet slogan for a title, and now it's called Power to the Grid. Palych lost his exclamation marks. In English he comes out drier, and it suits him. There's a footnote on one of the forms joking that you won't be going to Egypt on holiday any more, since people with a security clearance weren't allowed abroad. Egypt means nothing outside Russia, so now it says: "with your clearance, beach holidays abroad are off the table anyway."

Palych is still Palych. It's a nickname made from his patronymic, and the game explains it once.

Nothing about the reactor has changed: same physics, same shifts, same key under the seal.

If a line reads oddly, a typo or something no English speaker would ever say, please leave a comment. I'd like to hear about it.
