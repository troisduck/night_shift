# Night Shift

**Tagline (itch.io short description):**
A small nuclear control room game for people who have never seen one. Russian language only, for now.

---

**Russian language only, for now.** All the explaining is done in text, so you'll need to read Russian.

The village runs on the plant at its edge. Every lit window and every warm radiator depends on the reactor. The people who started it up are past sixty and about to retire. Nobody is lined up to take over except you, the one kid from around here who went away and studied nuclear physics.

Tonight is your first night shift. The ceiling lights come on row by row, the window is dark, and Palych, the shift supervisor, is already in his chair. He'll explain things as they come up. He's in no hurry, and you shouldn't be either.

## What you do

- Ease the control rods up and wait. Power takes its time to follow, and the startup rate needle moves first. Rush it and the reactor trips.
- Open the turbine valve and send 600 MW to the grid. Through the control room window you can watch the village light up.
- Lose the grid in a thunderstorm. The reactor shuts down, but the core keeps making heat on its own. Get the backup diesels and the pumps going before the water gets too hot.
- Try to restart after a trip and find out why the reactor won't come back up. (Xenon. Palych will explain.)

Nine shifts in all, ending with a full night from 20:00 to 08:00, the dispatcher calling in load changes and a pump that picks its own moment to fail. After the ninth there is one more: the same night with the automation switched off.

Alarms flash and ring until you acknowledge them. Pumps hum, relays click, the rod drives grind. Best with headphones.

Under a lead seal on the emergency protection panel there is a key. Turn it, and the automation will no longer shut the reactor down, whatever happens. Palych will tell you he never saw the seal. The key is there from the first shift. You can wreck the reactor, and how it goes depends on what you got wrong.

The physics is simplified and time runs fast. The cause and effect are real.

## Details

- Language: Russian
- Plays in the browser. It's also a single HTML file you can download and run offline
- Keyboard or mouse
- The first few shifts take 15–20 minutes. Getting through all nine without a slip takes longer
