# Night Shift

**Tagline (itch.io short description):**
A small nuclear control room game for people who have never seen one.

---

The town runs on the plant, and the people who started it up are about to retire. You're the only one around who studied nuclear physics. Tonight is your first night shift, and Palych, the shift supervisor, will explain things as they come up.

Raise the rods and wait. Power takes its time, and if you rush it, the reactor scrams. Send 600 MW to the grid and watch the town light up through the window. Ride out a pump failure and a blackout in a thunderstorm, then find out why the reactor won't come back up after a scram.

Under a lead seal there's a key that bypasses the protection. "I didn't see the seal," says Palych.

Nine shifts, and one more with the automation off. The physics is simplified, the cause and effect are real. English and Russian. Best with headphones.
