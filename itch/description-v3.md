# Night Shift

**Tagline (itch.io short description):**
A small nuclear control room game for people who have never seen one.

---

The town runs on the plant. The people who started it up are past sixty and about to retire, and the only one left to take over is you, the kid who went away and studied nuclear physics. Tonight is your first night shift. Palych, the shift supervisor, is already in his chair. He'll explain things as they come up.

- Ease the control rods up and wait. Power takes its time to follow. Rush it and the reactor scrams.
- Open the turbine valve and send 600 MW to the grid. Through the window you can watch the town light up.
- Lose the grid in a thunderstorm. The reactor shuts down but keeps making heat. Get the diesels and pumps going before the water gets too hot.
- Try to restart after a scram and find out why the reactor won't come back up.

Nine shifts, ending with a full night from 20:00 to 08:00. After that, one more with the automation switched off.

Under a lead seal on the protection panel there is a protection bypass key. Turn it, and nothing will shut the reactor down. "I didn't see the seal," says Palych. The key is there from the first shift, and how the reactor goes depends on what you got wrong.

Alarms ring until you acknowledge them, pumps hum, relays click. Best with headphones.

The physics is simplified and time runs fast. The cause and effect are real.

**Details**
- English and Russian (picked from your browser, switchable in settings)
- Plays in the browser; also a single HTML file that runs offline
- Keyboard or mouse
- First shifts take 15–20 minutes
